# gta6-clipper-01 — GTA 6 Launch Trailer clip

## The clip
`clip.mp4` — 26 seconds, vertical 1080x1920 (YouTube Shorts format).
A real cut of Rockstar Games footage, edited with ffmpeg. Not generated imagery.

## The moment chosen
The action montage from Rockstar's official **Grand Theft Auto VI Launch Trailer**
(15 Oct 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rz1B4B2yBZY — 85M+ views,
captured entirely in-game on PS5). The cut uses trailer time **0:47–1:08**:

- opens on a green supercar speeding through Vice City (instant GTA hook, frame 1)
- a motorbike stunt jump off a rooftop at ~0:50 (the most spectacular shot)
- Jason with fireworks, the pink-smoke beach biplane, masked robbers,
  Lucia on a rooftop at sunset
- closes on Jason and Lucia's sunset embrace — the emotional payoff

Why this moment: it is the freshest official Rockstar footage (a month old, not
the 18-month-old Trailer 2 that has already been clipped to death), it is
downloadable from Rockstar's own YouTube upload, and the 21-second stretch is
pure action with a face, a stunt, or a shock in every 2–3 seconds — built for
Shorts retention. The launch-day opening gameplay was considered and rejected:
it only circulates as social re-uploads, not Rockstar-official, and Rockstar
asks that story-revealing gameplay not be re-uploaded.

Format choice: the trailer is letterboxed 2:1 (1920x960 active inside 1080p),
so a centre crop to 9:16 would slice faces off — the clip instead uses a
blurred-background pillarbox, keeping every shot whole.
